What this tool actually does

This is a statistical screening aid, not a diagnostic device. It compares the numbers you enter to patterns found in a research dataset and shows which pattern they resemble more closely. It cannot see your full medical history, run any tests, or examine you — only a qualified clinician can do that. Use the result as a conversation starter with your doctor, never as an answer on its own.

If you're having symptoms right now, don't wait for a tool

Chest pain or pressure, shortness of breath, or sudden pain spreading to your arm, jaw, or back can be a medical emergency. Call your local emergency number immediately. See the warning signs page for the full list.
