Heart Health Guide brings together clear, sourced information about heart disease risk factors, warning signs, and prevention — written for real people, not textbooks. It's a starting point for your own questions, never a replacement for the person who knows your health best: your doctor.

What this site is — and isn't

This site explains general heart health concepts in plain language and offers a screening tool that estimates statistical risk patterns. It cannot examine you, review your full medical history, or run tests — so it cannot diagnose heart disease or any other condition. If something here raises a concern, the next right step is always a conversation with a qualified healthcare professional, not a decision made from this site alone.
